About GECOD

Supporting girls to learn, lead and thrive

Girls’ Empowerment for Community Development (GECOD) works with schools and communities to support girls’ education, menstrual health, protection and leadership.

Across refugee and host communities, girls face barriers that go beyond the classroom. When sanitation is unsafe, school attendance drops and confidence fades. GECOD responds with practical, community-led solutions that keep girls in school and protected.

★

School support

Safe, clean and accessible facilities that help girls stay in class.

✓

Menstrual health

Education and dignity support that reduces stigma and improves attendance.

#

Protection

Community action that makes girls feel safe, respected and able to thrive.

Why Ayivu

Keep girls learning at Ayivu

01 / THE NEED

Safe sanitation matters

Ayivu’s schools serve refugee and host-community girls in Bidibidi Refugee Settlement, Yumbe District, Uganda. When a school has no safe toilet for girls, a period can mean days away from class every month.

02 / THE RESPONSE

Permanent facilities, lasting impact

A clean, private toilet close by helps girls stay in class. GECOD works with schools and communities on menstrual hygiene, girls’ education and protection from violence. These two blocks make that work part of the school environment.
