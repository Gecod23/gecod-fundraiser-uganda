Why Ayivu

Schools that serve refugee and host-community girls

Ayivu’s need

Ayivu’s schools serve refugee and host-community girls in Bidibidi Refugee Settlement, Yumbe District, Uganda. When a school has no safe toilet for girls, a period can mean days away from class every month.

Long-term impact

A toilet that is clean, private and close by keeps girls learning. GECOD works with schools and communities on menstrual hygiene, girls’ education and protection from violence, and these two blocks put that work into permanent facilities.
